Bottom line

Proceed with conditions. Intellectus does not currently make significant automated decisions about people. Its principal GDPR risk is that public organisational research can include identifiable people, sensitive information and AI-generated inferences. Production should wait for a confirmed role/vendor map, approved legitimate-interests and Article 14 approach, strict purpose and sensitive-data controls, Article 28 and transfer terms, secured webhook architecture, n8n/vendor retention rules, tested individual-rights workflows and formal consultant review.
